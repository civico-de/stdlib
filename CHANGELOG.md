# Changelog

## 0.1.3 (2026-11-04)

- `Cast::toStringOrNull` and `Cast::toBool`.
- `Email::key`.

## 0.1.2 (2026-11-04)

- `Expect::int` accepts zero-padded integer strings such as `'007'`.

## 0.1.1 (2026-11-04)

- `Expect` with `int`, `str`, `optStr`, `bool`, `optFloat`, `row` and `timestamp`.

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-12)

- `Cast` with `toString`, `toInt`, `toFloat`, `toStoredString` and `namedOnly`.
